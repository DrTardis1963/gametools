What makes a slime chunk

The world is cut into chunks of 16 × 16 blocks. About one chunk in ten is a slime chunk, where slimes can spawn below Y = 40 at any light level and in any biome (swamps and mangrove swamps have their own surface spawning as well). To find your chunk, divide your X and Z by 16 and round down: block X = 24 is in chunk 1, and block X = −24 is in chunk −2.

Java Edition picks slime chunks from the world seed. The game mixes the seed with the chunk's two coordinates into a starting number for Java's random number generator, draws one number from 0 to 9, and makes the chunk a slime chunk if that number is 0. Only the lower 48 bits of the seed matter, and a seed that is text rather than a number is first turned into a number the same way the game does it. Change the seed and the slime chunks move.

Bedrock Edition ignores the seed. It seeds a different generator (a Mersenne Twister) from the chunk coordinates alone, so slime chunks are in the same places in every Bedrock world.

Worked example

In the Java world with seed 146008555, standing at X = 24, Z = −24 puts you in chunk (1, −2): 24 ÷ 16 = 1.5 → 1, and −24 ÷ 16 = −1.5 → −2. That chunk is a slime chunk. It covers blocks X 16 to 31 and Z −32 to −17. Chunk (−2, 2) is another; chunk (0, 0) is not.

On Bedrock, chunk (3, 1), covering X 48 to 63 and Z 16 to 31, is a slime chunk in every world, and so is chunk (−61, −59). Chunk (0, 0) and chunk (−61, −60) are not.

Checking it in game

  • Press F3 + G (Java) to show chunk borders, and F3 to see your chunk number on the "Chunk" line.
  • Dig a room below Y = 40 in the chunk, at least 3 blocks high, and wait. In Java, slimes spawn in a slime chunk at any light level. If none show up after a few minutes in a big lit-up area, check the seed: a server's seed is not the seed of your own single-player copy.
  • Spawning needs a player 24 to 128 blocks away. Stand about 30 blocks above the room, not inside it.
  • Join neighbouring slime chunks into one bigger farm floor when you can; the map makes clusters easy to see.

Is the seed safe to type here?

Yes. Everything is worked out in your browser; the seed is not sent anywhere. On a server, ask an admin before using its seed: some servers treat seed-based maps as cheating.
